About Me

Explore the World of Donna Mae

Growing up in a family of teachers, I learned early that knowledge becomes more meaningful when it is shared. I've always been a vessel for new ideas and experiences, with an instinct to take what I absorb, unravel it until I understand it deeply, and find my own way of giving it back through words. Over the years, writing has found me in many forms including songwriting, poetry, blogging, research, technical writing, and storytelling. Each has taught me something different, but they all lead me back to the same purpose, which is to inspire, educate, connect, and sometimes, even heal. To me, writing has never been simply a form of expression. It is a way of reaching across the distance between one mind and another. I write for the freedom to create, the joy of discovery, and the possibility that something I've written might stay with someone long after they've finished reading it. That is what makes my soul dance; turning curiosity into words and letting creativity flirt with the mind.
